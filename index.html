Dissolved Carbon Chemistry

CO₂(aq) ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ HCO₃⁻ ⇌ CO₃²⁻
When CO₂ dissolves in water, carbon can exist in several dissolved forms. Under suitable chemical conditions, carbonate ions, CO₃²⁻, can become available for mineral formation.
Scientific accuracy: CO₂ does not automatically become carbonate. The balance between the dissolved carbon species depends on the chemical conditions of the solution, especially pH.
